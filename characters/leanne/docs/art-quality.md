# Asset method and visual checks

Built-in image_gen mode. The original reference supplies design and pose intent. Seven generated whole-body sheets use an identical neutral as the sole scale reference; each sheet receives one uniform scale. Individual poses are never fitted by height. No joints, limb warps or regenerated locked originals.

Basic sheet: inspected full RGBA source, composited on solid dark blue, and extracted neutral. Face, boots, outfit hems and gun outlines are fully finished. Adult natural proportions and the black/gray-blue costume match the supplied reference. Source contains a genuine alpha channel; dark-gray appearance in an image viewer is transparent RGB matte, not painted opaque backdrop. Source PNG is preserved exactly.

Attack draft: image generation produced17 figures rather than required16. A targeted grid-layout correction was requested before locking, while preserving the finished design and action intent. The unselected draft remains as provenance only; it is not packed for game use.

Final inspection: corrected attack sheet has exactly16 complete figures; original neutral plus15 strikes were then fixed and hashed before windup/recovery generation. All locked PNGs pass byte-hash checks. The extra sheet's five cropped standing figures were completed with both boots and repacked before acceptance. The dedicated portrait crown was completed and reframed with transparent top margin. Native-scale contact sheets compare every whole-body pose using one physical scale, and transition sheets compare neutral→windup→locked strike→recovery for all15 normals. Distinct directional gunfire, gunstock, grounded leg sweep, slide, aerial aim, recoil, grenade and unarmed defense/throw poses remain visible.

Final atlas:107 frames (0–105 whole body,106 dedicated portrait) and16 FX. FX14 replaces the initial charge-reticle draft with a separately generated complete native green grenade. Reticle6 serves both aim and charge. Orange handgun/MG streaks and charged cyan bolt point right; FX2 points up,3 down; recoil15 opens right. Abstract resonance13 is not a weapon slash. Mirroring/rotation belongs to the move-aware runtime. No slash-curve convex-side normalization is needed for these gun/explosion assets.

`tests/native-points.json` records visually observed normalized crop-local gun grip/muzzle or direct limb contacts, in source-facing orientation (x right,y down). These are artwork observations for runtime alignment, not final hitboxes. Frames18 (gunstock),21/25 (boots) use direct contact endpoints; frame22 also records the extended leg. 34/43 are empty pointing/release hands,41/42 are grenade hand/release positions. Directional shooting points include31–33,35–39,44. No projectile or grenade area is assigned melee coverage by the asset pipeline.
